Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trenndly (“we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you use our website and our AI-powered YouTube SEO tool. Trenndly lets you upload a video and get suggested titles, tags, hashtags and keywords for it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, username, display name and profile picture, provided when you sign in with Google or create an account with email and password. If you sign in with Google, we only receive your basic profile information (name, email and profile picture). We do not access your Gmail, Drive, YouTube channel or any other Google data.
  • Videos you upload: the video file you choose to analyze, plus its file name, size and type.
  • Results and history: the titles, tags, hashtags and keywords generated for your video, the file name, and a small preview image taken from your video. These are shown in your History page.
  • Usage and analytics: a random visitor ID stored in your browser, your device type (mobile or desktop), and a record of each analysis (file name, size, whether it worked, and how long it took). This record is linked to your account email and name.
  • Feedback: any message you send through the feedback form, together with your email, username, the page you were on and your browser details.
  • Browser storage: small items saved on your device, such as your theme choice and whether you have seen the tutorial.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to sign you in, analyze your video and show you the results, save your History, enforce daily usage limits, prevent abuse, fix errors, understand how many people use the service, reply to your feedback, and keep Trenndly secure.

3. Video Uploads

Your video is sent through our server to Google's Gemini AI service, which watches it and listens to its audio in order to write the suggestions. We delete the video from Gemini as soon as the analysis finishes, whether it succeeded or failed. If something goes wrong before that, uploaded files expire automatically within about one hour. We do not keep your video file on our own servers.

Because the video is processed by an AI provider, please do not upload private, confidential or sensitive footage.

4. How Long We Keep Information

  • History: results and preview images are kept for 7 days and then deleted. You can also delete any History item yourself at any time.
  • Account details and username: kept until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Analytics and feedback records: kept for as long as needed to run and improve the service, unless you ask us to delete them.

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on these services to run Trenndly:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) for sign-in and for storing account details, History, analytics and feedback.
  • Google Gemini to analyze your video and write the suggestions.
  • Cloudflare (Workers and KV) to run our server code and to keep short-lived upload and usage-limit records.
  • Github to host the website.
  • YouTube search suggestions (a public Google service). We send short search phrases related to your video topic, not your video or your personal information.

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers listed above so they can run the service, or when the law requires it, or to investigate abuse and protect the safety and rights of our users and Trenndly.

7. Security

Sensitive actions such as checking your login, usernames, usage limits and video uploads are handled on our server, not in your browser. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can promise perfect security.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You can delete individual History items inside the app. To delete your account or ask about the information we hold about you, email us at the address below. We may need to confirm it is really you before we act, and we may keep some records where the law or security requires it.

9. Children's Privacy

Trenndly is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will review it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.
